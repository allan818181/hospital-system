# Hospital Management System

## 📘 Description
**Hospital Management System** (Meditrack) is a complete healthcare management platform designed to streamline patient care, medication tracking, and caregiver coordination.  
The system allows healthcare providers, patients, and caregivers to manage treatment plans, schedules, and health records efficiently and securely.

This system supports both **in-hospital and at-home care**, ensuring caregivers can assist patients effectively and patients can follow their medication schedules reliably.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- 🔒 **User Authentication & Role Management**  
  - Separate access for Admin, Patient, and Caregiver roles  

- 💊 **Medication Management**  
  - Schedule, track, and monitor medications for patients  

- 🏥 **Patient Care Tracking**  
  - Monitor patient health and treatment progress  

- 👨‍⚕️ **Caregiver Functionality**  
  - Assign caregivers to patients  
  - Manage at-home care tasks  

- 📊 **Dashboard & Reports**  
  - View overall patient stats, medication adherence, and caregiver activity  

- 🔄 **Collaboration & Notifications**  
  - Keep staff and caregivers informed of updates or critical alerts
